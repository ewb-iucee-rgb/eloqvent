Origins & Mission

What is ELOQVENT?

Where bold voices meet bold ideas.

ELOQVENT 2K26 is a two-day multidisciplinary platform that brings together communication, critical thinking, innovation, and entrepreneurship. It creates a space for participants to express perspectives, explore challenges, and approach problems with fresh thinking.

Through Elocution and Root Riddle, participants strengthen their communication skills while exploring human-centred problem-solving and solution development. From expressing a thought to uncovering the root of a problem, ELOQVENT encourages participants to think deeper, create smarter, and make meaningful impact.
